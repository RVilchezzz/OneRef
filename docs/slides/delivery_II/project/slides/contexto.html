Context

One good photo, one part, a question

1 · Visual expert

2 · Two readers

3 · Verifier

4 · Vote

Benchmark

MMAD: 8,366 images, 39,670 multiple-choice questions, 7 subtasks, strict 1-shot

Baseline to beat

Qwen3-VL-8B alone, 73.9%, and a same-cost majority vote

Rules

No training, models ≤ 8B, tune on dev only, test run once

Hypothesis: the multi-agent system beats both baselines with significance (McNemar test). A well-measured negative result is also valid.

Quick reminder of the project. A production line switches products and only one photo of a good part exists. A team of open pre-trained models inspects the new part: a visual expert compares it with the reference and points where to look, two MLLM readers answer the question, a verifier checks consistency and a vote gives the final answer. Nothing is trained. We evaluate on MMAD and must beat Qwen3-VL-8B alone and a majority vote with the same number of calls.
