Each card: reference · part · zoom the expert sent to the readers (orange). From the 18-image run: MVTec AD cable and tile, GoodsAD can, MVTec LOCO pushpins.

Four real cases from the run, chosen to show four different behaviours. Cable: the expert found the missing wires and the readers got all five questions right. Tile: the readers placed the stain bottom-left while the expert said bottom-center, so the verifier reopened that question once; the answer did not change and it was still wrong, which shows the reopen mechanism works but does not fix everything. Can: a good beer can was called defective because the only reference is a can of another brand. Pushpins: a logical defect, a missing separator; the reader wrote that the expert's hint pointed to the top right and simply followed it, to the wrong place. The lesson is that the expert's hint is strong in both directions, so in the final system its weight will depend on how reliable it was on dev.
