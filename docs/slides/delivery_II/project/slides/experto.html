Frozen DINOv2-base and DINOv3 ViT-B/16, query vs random_templates[0], dev split only, one run. RTX 3060 Laptop: 0.35 s per image. Masks used only to score.

These are our first real measurements: the visual expert alone on the 835 dev images, no training. The score is the distance between each patch of the part and its closest patch in the reference. On MVTec AD and VisA, where photos are aligned, it separates good and bad parts well, and DINOv3 puts its hottest point on the true defect 77% of the time on MVTec AD. On GoodsAD it is at chance level. One run on dev only, so small differences may be noise.
