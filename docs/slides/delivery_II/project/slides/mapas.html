Reference · part · our DINOv3 map

✓ Worked: defect found

✗ Failed: reference is another product

Real dev images: MVTec AD grid, VisA chewing gum, GoodsAD food box, MVTec LOCO juice. Images: MMAD (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, CC BY 4.0, research use).

Left, what worked: on the metal grid and the chewing gum, the map's hot spot sits exactly on the defect. Right, what went wrong: in GoodsAD the single reference is a green box while the part is a pink box, so everything lights up; in MVTec LOCO the reference is mango juice and the part is banana juice, so the map flags the label, which is a normal variation, not the defect. The expert cannot tell variation from defect: this is exactly the readers' and the verifier's job, and a reason to weight the expert by how reliable it is.
